Good to know

Questions drivers ask

Can you give me a price before I bring the car in?

Yes. Send clear photos of the damage on WhatsApp, or use the instant price tool on our Pricing page. We confirm the final price after a quick inspection, before any work starts.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Yes, we handle accident repairs for insured vehicles. Bring your claim details and we'll work through the assessment with you.

How long does a full respray take?

It depends on the car and the finish, but we'll give you a timeline with your quote and keep you posted with progress photos.

Do you use genuine parts?

Always. We source authentic parts from trusted suppliers and tell you exactly what goes into your car.

Are you open on public holidays?

Yes. We work Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 5 pm, including public holidays. Sundays we rest.

Where is the car wash?

Our car wash branch is in Airport North Road, Embakasi. Drop in for a snow-foam wash and interior clean while you wait.
